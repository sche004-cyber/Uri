**Verdict: REJECT.** There are three HIGH findings. Neither structural change holds.

### HIGH findings
- **R1-01: canonical egress puts a full card on the wire.** This is a regression of R2-01. `canonical()` deletes invisible characters before `classify()` runs, so a card next to a digit becomes one longer, unflagged run.
  - Wire, WEB_SEARCH: `card 4111111111111111\u200b9 declined` is sent as `card 41111111111111119 declined` with status NO_RESULTS.
  - Same leak at EXTERNAL_AGENT, EXTERNAL_MODEL and LOG. Prefix digits, U+00AD and Aadhaar `234567890124\u200b5` behave the same way.
  - Classifying the raw text gives FINANCIAL plus LONG_NUMBER; classifying the canonical text gives nothing.
- **R1-02: the "arbitrary separators" claim is false.** The cause is `_NUM_RUN` (separator `[^\w\n]{1,3}`), its exclusions, and `_CARD_SEP`. Wire leaks:
  - `card 4111****1111****1111****1111`
  - `card 4,111,111,111,111,111` (thousands exclusion)
  - `aadhaar_2345_6789_0124.pdf` (underscore counts as `\w`; a realistic filename)
  - `aadhaar 2345__6789__0124`
  - `aadhaar 234.567.890.124` (IPv4 exclusion)
  - Gateway-only leaks: `2345 - - 6789 - - 0124`, `234,567,890,124`, letter separators. Literal `%` separators also leak (`4111%1111...`) because every reading decodes `%11` and swallows digits. No reading checks the literal text that is actually sent.
- **R1-03: punctuation look-alikes are not folded.** The confusables table maps letters and digits only (U+2010, U+2011, U+2212, U+2236, U+3002 all map to None). Hard-refuse credentials therefore reach WEB_SEARCH, EXTERNAL and LOG raw:

  - Gateway only: `password\ua789 ...`, `password\u2011 ...`, `xoxb\u2010...`.
  - Also raw: SSN `ssn 123\u201045\u20106789` (wire) and `123\u221245\u22126789`, phones `415\u2010555\u20100199` and `98765\u201043210`, e-mail `john@example\u3002com`.
