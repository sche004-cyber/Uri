Findings
R1-01 HIGH, blocking, STRUCTURAL. gateway.py:126-137 reconciles the egress-form reading with the original reading by
  category COUNT (Counter difference). It never compares positions. A decoy number that both readings see, and that
  the original reading merges with the disguised value into one finding, makes the counts equal. The egress form is
  then sent with only the decoy redacted:
    'ref 123456789 41\u06d4111\u06d4111\u06d4111:11111' -> 'ref [redacted] 41.111.111.111:11111'
  This happens at WEB_SEARCH (Tavily MockTransport received exactly that query), EXTERNAL_AGENT, EXTERNAL_MODEL and LOG.
  Generated sweep: 5468 of 6000 card texts and 726 of 1000 Aadhaar texts leaked with a decoy. Without a decoy, 0 of 600
  leaked, which means the FB8 R2-02 repair holds only when the egress reading finds nothing at all.
R1-02 MEDIUM, blocking, STRUCTURAL. If the egress form contains an Assist credential, gateway.py:126 skips the original
  reading entirely. At LOG the egress form is then written out with only that credential redacted:
    'Authorization: Bearer abcDEF123456ghiJKL789012mnoPQR 41\u06d4111\u06d4111\u06d4111:11111'
      -> 'Authorization: [redacted] 41.111.111.111:11111'
R1-03 LOW RECALL. Basic auth whose base64 has no digit or padding is sent ('Authorization: Basic dXNlcjpwYXNz').
R1-04 LOW RECALL. Labelled 7-8 digit recovery/backup codes are sent ('recovery code 12345678').
R1-05 LOW RECALL. A defanged e-mail keeps its first local-part label ('john[.]doe[@]example[.]com' -> 'john[.][redacted]').
R1-06 LOW documentation (owner decision). The AC-14c evidence never exercises the two-reading path. With one NBSP in a
  64 KB text, p95 was 0.81-1.07 s for ASCII, 1.21 s for Arabic and 1.12 s for Hindi. The plan says 64 KB is met for
  ASCII. The 400-character and 2 KB margins are thin too: 9.9 ms against 10 ms, and 48 ms against 50 ms.
R1-07 LOW documentation. The egress map leaves out web-research URL and feed fetches: user URLs go to third-party
  hosts without the gateway.
