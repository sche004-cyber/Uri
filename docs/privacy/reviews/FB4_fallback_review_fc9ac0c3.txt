  - Cause: `_STD_RUN` (classify.py:177) only matches groups of at most six digits and at least three groups, and `_AADHAAR` only accepts 4-4-4.
  - I generated every 2–4 group split of a card with 10 standard separators and the label "card". 38.7% of them leak, and 31.2% of a labelled Aadhaar's splits leak.

    - `my aadhaar is 234567 890124` (6-6 with a plain space)
  - The scheduler harness path also passes them through.
  - This contradicts 6.3 row 2.
- **R1-02 (HIGH).** The disguise check for 9+ digits joined by unusual separators or letters is evaded by mixing separator kinds or by a newline, because the run splits into pieces under 9 digits.
