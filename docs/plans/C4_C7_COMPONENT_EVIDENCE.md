`core/client_execution_control.py` provides prepare, claim, cancel, settle and restart/deadline reconciliation. Trusted composition must inject canonical authority/approval/grant/adapter-qualification and deterministic receipt-verification callbacks; defaults refuse. It is NOT mounted on HTTP or wired to a dispatcher. The claim's flushed atomic control record precedes acknowledgement. Identical acknowledgement replay explicitly returns dispatch=false. Revocation, expiry, generation, ownership and CAS are rechecked. A claimed cancellation, deadline or server restart becomes EXECUTION_UNCERTAIN rather than permission to rerun. The ledger persists only minimal bindings/digests/state, not arguments or evidence.

`client_runtime/execution_journal.py` records claim intent before online acknowledgement, rejects duplicate/conflicting intent and unexpected receipts, and preserves uncertainty after lost acknowledgement/restart. Signature/scope validation is required through a trusted callback; no default validation or native executor exists. This is separate from reversible C3 store transactions; restoring store files cannot undo external side effects.

`client_runtime/process_tree.py` selectively adapts the preserved `uri-m37-m39-integration/uri_v1/execution/proctree.py`, with donor hash recorded locally. No branch was merged.
