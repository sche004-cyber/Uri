Earlier records said the art "appears to be a third-party Fiverr illustration" and the reference provenance file
(`docs/companions/fire/reference/PROVENANCE.md`, commit `8a1438ec`) says the screenshot is an "image search result
attributed to a Fiverr illustration listing". Nothing in the repository says the art was **commissioned** from Fiverr;
no record claims a purchase. Corrected statement: the repository's only evidence about the first image is a phone
screenshot of ChatGPT that shows a Fiverr source card under it (section 3). Whether that image was generated by ChatGPT
or retrieved from the web is not established. The words "Fiverr illustration" / "commissioned" should not be used as
facts.

Independently copyrighted third-party artwork: **cannot be excluded.** If the first image is a Fiverr seller's gig
image (as its source card indicates), the Idle panel and every atlas derived from it would derive from that seller's
work, regardless of the later AI refinement. A signature-like mark is a further indicator of a human-signed original,
though image generators can also produce pseudo-signatures. If instead ChatGPT generated it, the mark is a model
artefact and the chain is AI-only.

## 5. Applicable terms (summary, to be confirmed against current official text)

- OpenAI Terms of Use: assign to the user OpenAI's rights in Output "if any"; outputs may be similar for other users;
  copyright in AI output depends on national law (e.g. US human-authorship requirement). This covers only content
  OpenAI generated - not third-party web content shown in ChatGPT search results.
- Google Gemini (API Additional Terms; consumer app terms not retrieved): Google does not claim ownership of generated
  content; may generate similar content for others; the user is responsible for lawful use. An image-to-image result
  does not remove rights a third party holds in the input image.

## 6. What would resolve OD-2 (owner-held evidence; no asset change needed)

1. The ChatGPT conversation `chatgpt.com/c/6acef...` (or a ChatGPT data export of it) showing the image as a generated
   output (the prompt and the generated image, not a search result), or
2. a reverse image search of the screenshot image (e.g. Google Lens / TinEye) showing no earlier third-party source,
   ideally with the Fiverr category page checked for the image, and
3. the Gemini conversation that produced the sheet (input image + prompt).

With (1) or (2)+(3), OD-2 can be closed as "AI-generated chain; residual: AI-output copyright limitations and the
inpainted pseudo-signature". If (2) finds the image on a Fiverr gig, the owner needs that seller's licence (or the
art is replaced - only then).
