**R14-01 (HIGH): links built from stroke radicals still get through**
- **What fails:** `'refunds-portal 丨丶 shop/claim'` is not detected as a link. The same holds with `一丶`, `丶一` and `乙丶`, and with the Kangxi radical forms `⼁⼂` and `⼀⼂`. My probe test sent all six through `has_link_or_contact`, `mask_links`, `verify()`, `extractive()`, `build_surface` and `require_valid`, and all 18 checks failed. The link text reaches the research surface unmasked and the A2UI validator accepts it.
- **Why:**
  - Text is NFKC-normalised before the check (`uri_web/untrusted.py:255`). That turns every Kangxi radical (U+2F00–U+2FD5) into its ordinary CJK character. The range listed in `_STROKES` (`uri_web/untrusted.py:516`) therefore never matches. This includes `⼂` (U+2F02), the form R9-01 named.
  - The single-stroke radicals 一 (U+4E00), 丨 (U+4E28) and 乙 (U+4E59) are missing from `_STROKES`. So a word made of two different stroke letters is read as the name of the site rather than as a separator.
- **Scope:** D17 says stroke and dot-shaped letters, including CJK stroke and dot radicals, count as separators. The owner-accepted residuals are only a bracketed separator and a real short foreign word. `丨丶` is neither. So this is the R13-01 defect itself ("two distinct dot-shaped letters read as the name"), not an accepted exception.
- **Suggested fix:**
  - Build `_STROKES` from the NFKC forms of U+2F00–U+2FD5, or apply the same normalisation to the set.
  - Add 一, 丨, 乙 and 亅 explicitly.
  - Add a self-contained regression test covering the cases above.

- I did not run the Flutter suite or the Playwright browser tests.
