4. RAR Authority Policy

Audit and enforce the following distinction.

Hard/High-Certainty Binding

May directly provide a resolved anchor when unique and mechanically supported:

- exact ID
- exact alias
- current attachment
- active UI selection
- unique exact title
- unique schema/type-constrained candidate

Conditional Binding

May resolve only when metadata makes the result unique and non-conflicting:

- active pointer
- contrast elimination
- temporal relation
- revision relation

Ranking Only

TF-IDF / discriminating-term overlap should normally narrow or rank candidates.

Do NOT silently bind solely because one candidate receives the highest lexical score unless an already-defined deterministic uniqueness rule independently justifies it.

If confidence is insufficient:

UNKNOWN
or
AMBIGUOUS

then pass the bounded candidate set forward.
